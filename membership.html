Why Join Gandaki Orthopaedic Association?

Becoming a member of GOA provides you with a platform to connect with like-minded orthopaedic professionals, enhance your knowledge, and contribute to advancing orthopaedic care in the Gandaki region.

Resources

Access to exclusive educational and technical medical materials.

Research

Engage in peer-reviewed orthopedic studies and regional research loops.

Workshops

Guaranteed invitations and options to participate in regular regional CMEs.

Enrollment Options

Membership Categories

Premium Tier

Life Members

Exclusive to Orthopaedic Surgeons

Life membership is a one-time enrollment, granting lifetime access to all benefits, voting rights, and official representations within the association.

Requires valid NMC Speciality Registration in Orthopaedics.
Training Tier

Associate Members

For Postgraduate Trainees

Designed for postgraduate residents currently pursuing orthopaedic training, preferably in the Gandaki region. Associate members gain vital access to academic resources, workshops, and networking opportunities.

Applicable for residents in recognized post-graduate programs.

Process

How to Apply

Step 01

Verify Eligibility

Life: Orthopaedic surgeons with valid NMC Speciality Registration.

Associate: Post-graduate residents in recognized programs.

Step 02

Participation Requirement

Attend at least one bi-monthly CME or academic gathering organized by GOA to build your presence in the council loop.

Step 03

Form Enrollment

Membership forms will be available explicitly during these regular academic events, offering a chance to engage directly with peers and experts.

Step 04

Submission & Review

Complete the physical form during the gathering and fulfill all background administrative documentation requirements.
