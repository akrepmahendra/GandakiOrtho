About GOA

Fostering Clinical Excellence and Advancing Orthopedic Care in the region.

The Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA) is a professional organization established in 2018 AD to advance the field of Orthopaedics in the Gandaki region of Nepal. We are committed to fostering excellence in orthopaedic care through collaboration, education, research, and innovation.

Our association serves as a hub for orthopaedic professionals, offering opportunities to share knowledge, refine skills, and contribute to the betterment of patient outcomes. Through CME, conferences, and public health initiatives, GOA is dedicated to addressing the unique healthcare needs of the region while ensuring that high-quality orthopaedic care is accessible to all.

President's Word

Message to the Medical Fraternity

Respected Seniors, Esteemed Members and Colleagues,

It is my immense honor and privilege to serve as the President of this esteemed organization. As the President of the Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA), I am dedicated to advancing orthopedic education, research, clinical continuing medical education (CME), strengthening academic conferences, and fostering professional collaboration throughout the Gandaki region and beyond.

We firmly believe that continuous academic activities, such as symposiums, hands-on workshops, regular conferences, scientific discussions, and collaborative learning, are crucial for enhancing patient care and strengthening the orthopedic community.
