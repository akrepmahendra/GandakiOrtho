A Warm Welcome from the Organizers

Dear Esteemed Guests, Delegates, and Faculties,

On behalf of the Executive Committee of the Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA), led by President Dr. Chandra Bahadur Mishra, it is our absolute honor to extend a warm welcome to GOACON 2026.

As we gather under the banner of "Learning Innovation and Excellence Together," GOACON 2026 serves as a vital platform for sharing ground breaking surgical techniques, research findings, and clinical insights. GOA is proud to host distinguished national and international orthopaedic minds here in the picturesque valley of Pokhara to foster professional training, support continuous learning, advance research frameworks, and enhance clinical synergy.

By bringing together leading experts to consult on complex trauma cases and share data, this conference strengthens our integrated collaborative network — refining surgical precision and promoting early prevention and treatment for overall bone and joint health in our communities.

Pokhara the Gateway of Himalayas:Discover While You Attend

While the academic program promises rich scientific deliberations, we encourage all participants and accompanying family members to make time for Pokhara's natural beauty.

Watch Pokhara Preview

🌅 Lakes & Waterfalls

  • Phewa Lake: Take a tranquil wooden boat ride (doonga) to the Tal Barahi Temple on a central island, or enjoy the lakeside walk as Annapurna's peaks reflect off the water.
  • Begnas & Rupa Lakes: Serene spots a short drive away, perfect for quiet walks and local fish delicacies.
  • Devi's Fall & Gupteshwor Cave: Witness the roaring underground waterfall and explore the intricate limestone cave network.

🛕 Stupas & Temples

  • World Peace Pagoda: Perched high on Anadu Hill, offering panoramic views of the Annapurna range and Phewa Lake.
  • Pumdikot Shiva Statue: A massive monument featuring breathtaking 360-degree vistas of the valley.

🌄 Sarangkot Sunrise & Adventure

  • Sarangkot: The premier spot for early-morning views of sunrise lighting up Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) and the Annapurna range.
  • Paragliding: Glide over Sarangkot with high-flying views of the lake and mountains.
  • Zip-lining & Ultralight Flights: Adrenaline-fueled ways to take in the Himalayan landscape.

🌙 Lakeside Nightlife & Leisure

  • Lakeside Promenade: Stroll along vibrant pedestrian-friendly streets lined with cafes, artisan shops, and bookstores.
  • Live Music & Dining: Experience Lakeside's celebrated nightlife with live acoustic music, traditional dances, and international dining.

We wish you an enriching conference filled with insightful knowledge exchange, inspiring collaborations, and unforgettable memories in Pokhara!

About GOA

Fostering Clinical Excellence and Advancing Orthopedic Care in the region.

The Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA) is a professional organization established in 2018 AD to advance the field of Orthopaedics in the Gandaki region of Nepal. We are committed to fostering excellence in orthopaedic care through collaboration, education, research, and innovation.

Our association serves as a hub for orthopaedic professionals, offering opportunities to share knowledge, refine skills, and contribute to the betterment of patient outcomes. Through CME, conferences, and public health initiatives, GOA is dedicated to addressing the unique healthcare needs of the region while ensuring that high-quality orthopaedic care is accessible to all.

President's Word

Message to the Medical Fraternity

Respected Seniors, Esteemed Members and Colleagues,

It is my immense honor and privilege to serve as the President of this esteemed organization. As the President of the Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA), I am dedicated to advancing orthopedic education, research, clinical continuing medical education (CME), strengthening academic conferences, and fostering professional collaboration throughout the Gandaki region and beyond.

We firmly believe that continuous academic activities, such as symposiums, hands-on workshops, regular conferences, scientific discussions, and collaborative learning, are crucial for enhancing patient care and strengthening the orthopedic community.Through unity, innovation, and unwavering dedication, we aspire to promote evidence-based practice and inspire the next generation of orthopedic professionals.

I sincerely encourage all members to actively participate and contribute to GOA’s academic initiatives. Together, we can make significant strides in advancing orthopedic education and improving healthcare services in our region.

Thank you for your unwavering trust, support, and commitment to our shared vision.
