Our History

Rooted in Regional Progress

Established officially in 2018 AD, the Gandaki Orthopaedic Association (GOA) is the premier professional body dedicated to elevating bone and joint healthcare across the Gandaki region of Nepal and beyond.

GOA serves as a collaborative hub where orthopaedic surgeons and specialists connect to share knowledge, drive innovation, and continuously improve patient care.

"Through regular Continuing Medical Education (CME) seminars, localized workshops, and our flagship annual conference (GOACON), we bridge the gap between global innovations and local clinical practices."

Our Pillars

Core Objectives

Academic Excellence

Fostering professional training programs, research frameworks, and continuous learning systems to refine the surgical precision of local doctors.

Community Service

Organizing public health initiatives, Educating the public about bone and joint health to promote early prevention and treatment.

Professional Unity

Providing an integrated, collaborative network for local orthopaedic surgeons to share data, consult on complex trauma cases, and build professional synergy.
